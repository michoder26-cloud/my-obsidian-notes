# Doraemon The Movie Trailer: "Das chaotische Wolken-Vergnügungspark" (The Chaotic Cloud Amusement Park)

This document contains the complete script for a 120-second Doraemon Movie trailer (12 scenes, 10 seconds each) featuring official gadgets: **"The Anywhere Door" (ประตูสารพัดสถานที่)**, **"The Cloud Solidifying Spray" (สเปรย์เมฆแข็งตัว)**, **"The Bamboo Copter" (คอปเตอร์ไม้ไผ่)**, and **"The Weather Box" (กล่องเปลี่ยนสภาพอากาศ)**.

This version focuses on classic anime slapstick humor, transformations, funny expressions, and high-energy situations. Every scene features dynamic camera directions and strict negative prompts to prevent unwanted hand-waving gestures.

---

## 🎬 Movie Outline: "Das chaotische Wolken-Vergnügungspark"
**Total Duration:** 120 seconds (12 scenes, 10 seconds each)

*   **Scene 1 (0-10s):** Nobita is rolling on the floor, crying because Suneo bragged about a theme park. Doraemon sighs and pulls out **"The Anywhere Door"** and **"The Cloud Solidifying Spray"**.
*   **Scene 2 (10-20s):** Outside, Nobita steps through **"The Anywhere Door"** wearing a silly cartoon gold crown, bragging. Suneo bites his sleeve in comical jealousy. Doraemon smiles.
*   **Scene 3 (20-30s):** All five friends fly up with **"Bamboo Copters"** and see a giant amusement park made of solidified clouds (roller coaster, Ferris wheel).
*   **Scene 4 (30-40s) [Roller Coaster Chaos]:** Giant and Suneo ride the cloud roller coaster. Their carriage crashes into a soft cloud; Suneo gets comically stuck upside down with his legs wiggling. Nobita eats a giant cloud cotton candy larger than his head.
*   **Scene 5 (40-50s) [Cloud Transformations]:** The friends jump into a cloud pool and transform. Giant turns into a fluffy cloud gorilla beating his chest. Shizuka is a cute cloud kitten. Nobita is a fluffy cloud rabbit.
*   **Scene 6 (50-60s) [Floating Suneo]:** Suneo (transformed into a cloud fox) floats too high, drifting away like a balloon, his face looking funny and surprised.
*   **Scene 7 (60-70s) [The Rescue]:** Nobita and Doraemon fly up with Bamboo Copters. Nobita throws a glowing magic lasso rope, capturing Suneo's cloud tail gently to pull him back.
*   **Scene 8 (70-80s) [Jellyfish Crash]:** As they pull Suneo back, they accidentally crash into a giant, glowing neon-rainbow space cloud jellyfish floating in the air.
*   **Scene 9 (80-90s) [The Tickle Attack]:** The giant cloud jellyfish tickles them with its soft cloud tentacles. All five friends float in the air, laughing uncontrollably with tears of joy.
*   **Scene 10 (90-100s) [The Wind Boost]:** Doraemon uses the **"Weather Box"**, inserting the wind card. A strong wind blows the jellyfish away, and they land safely back on the cloud.
*   **Scene 11 (100-110s) [Giant's Concert]:** Around a glowing yellow stardust cloud campfire, Giant sings terribly into a cloud microphone, making a funny strained face. Nobita and Suneo plug their ears, grimacing.
*   **Scene 12 (110-120s) [Thai Drama Ending]:** A giant glowing star portal opens in the sky, and a golden key floats down. Doraemon catches it. As the portal glows intensely, Nobita's glasses tilt, his mouth open in happy shock. Screen freezes on Nobita's face and cuts to black abruptly!
