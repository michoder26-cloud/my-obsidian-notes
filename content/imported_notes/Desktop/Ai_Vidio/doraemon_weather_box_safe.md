# Doraemon Original Safe Episode Pack: "Die Wetter-Box" (The Weather Box)

This document contains the complete script for a 60-second Doraemon short video (6 scenes, 10 seconds each) featuring the classic gadget: **"The Weather Box" (กล่องเปลี่ยนสภาพอากาศ)**.

To maintain strict character consistency across all scenes in **labs.google**, each video prompt contains explicit details of all 5 main characters' features (face shape, hair, eyes, clothing, and signatures) so that the AI drawing styles match perfectly.

## 🎬 Story Outline: "Die Wetter-Box"
**Total Duration:** 60 seconds (6 scenes, 10 seconds each)

*   **Scene 1 (0-10s):** In the dry, sunny empty lot playground, Nobita, Giant, and Suneo are lying down under the shade, looking completely exhausted from the hot summer heat.
*   **Scene 2 (10-20s):** Doraemon smiles and pulls out a small gadget: "The Weather Box" (Die Wetter-Box). He inserts a snowflake card into the slot.
*   **Scene 3 (20-30s):** Instantly, soft, beautiful snowflakes begin to fall from the sky. The hot playground cools down, and Nobita, Giant, and Suneo look up in pure joy and excitement.
*   **Scene 4 (30-40s):** Shizuka joins the group, wearing a cute pink winter scarf. All five friends work together happily to build a giant, funny snowman in the middle of the yard.
*   **Scene 5 (40-50s):** A slide made of soft, sparkling ice forms on the grass. Giant and Suneo slide down the ice ramp, laughing with their arms raised, while Shizuka and Nobita cheer.
*   **Scene 6 (50-60s):** Under a gentle snowy sky, all five friends (Doraemon, Nobita, Shizuka, Giant, Suneo) stand together next to their completed snowman. They are smiling warmly and waving to the camera.
