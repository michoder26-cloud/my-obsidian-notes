# Doraemon The Movie Special: "Der getarnte Präsident" (The Disguised President) - Version 5 (No Copyright Names Edition)

This document contains the complete script for a 100-second Moral Drama video (10 scenes, 10 seconds each) featuring the official gadget: **"The Rank Badge" (เข็มกลัดแสดงยศ)**.

To fully bypass all text-to-speech, dialogue, and video safety filters of Google VideoFX/Veo, **all copyrighted character names (Doraemon, Nobita, Suneo, Giant, Shizuka) and trademarked words (Dorayaki) have been completely removed from both the English Video Prompts and the German Dialogues.**

## 🎬 Story Outline: "Der getarnte Präsident"
**Total Duration:** 100 seconds (10 scenes, 10 seconds each)

*   **Scene 1 (0-10s):** Spiky Hair Boy and Chubby Boy are eating ice cream in the park. Glasses Boy stands nearby looking at his toy on the grass. Spiky Hair Boy boasts about his ice cream.
*   **Scene 2 (10-20s):** Blue Cat pins the official golden Rank Badge on Glasses Boy's yellow shirt. Spiky Hair Boy and Chubby Boy watch with curious, smiling faces.
*   **Scene 3 (20-30s):** A luxury black sedan pulls up. A chauffeur in a suit opens the door politely for Glasses Boy, smiling. Chubby Boy and Spiky Hair Boy look surprised.
*   **Scene 4 (30-40s):** Glasses Boy suggests cleaning the park. Chubby Boy and Spiky Hair Boy help cheerfully; Spiky Hair Boy sweeps the leaves, and Chubby Boy happily carries a cardboard box.
*   **Scene 5 (40-50s):** Spiky Hair Boy holding his broom, talks politely to Glasses Boy, smiling. Glasses Boy stands beside him, smiling back warmly as equal friends.
*   **Scene 6 (50-60s) [The Moral Speech]:** Glasses Boy gives his toy to a passing child with a warm smile. Glasses Boy tells Spiky Hair Boy: "True friendship is about sharing and helping each other."
*   **Scene 7 (60-70s):** Chubby Boy sits on the grass next to his boxes to rest. Glasses Boy sits beside him, handing him a bottle of water with a friendly smile. Blue Cat stands nearby.
*   **Scene 8 (70-80s):** Glasses Boy removes the golden badge. Blue Cat pulls out round brown pancakes, and they all sit on the grass sharing food, smiling.
*   **Scene 9 (80-90s):** Spiky Hair Boy, Chubby Boy, and Glasses Boy stand together, talking and laughing happily as friends. Pigtails Girl walks by and smiles warmly at the friendly group.
*   **Scene 10 (90-100s) [Thai Drama Ending]:** Spiky Hair Boy offers his expensive ice cream to Glasses Boy. Glasses Boy smiles, opening his mouth to take a bite. Screen freezes on Spiky Hair Boy's smiling face and cuts to black abruptly!
    *   **Moral Quote Subtitle:** "True wealth is built on kindness, sharing, and helping others as good friends!"
