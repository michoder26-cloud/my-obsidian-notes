*   **Scene 10 (90-100s) [The Aquatic Boost]:** Doraemon inserts a glowing green "Aquatic Pearl" into a futuristic slot. The engine chamber glows with a brilliant neon green light, and the submarine shoots forward.
*   **Scene 11 (100-110s) [The Underwater Temple]:** The submarine glides towards a massive, glowing underwater toy palace made of colorful plastic crystals. The friends point and chat in awe.
*   **Scene 12 (110-120s) [Thai Drama Ending]:** Outside in miniature diving suits, they stand before a giant glowing shell. Doraemon opens it, releasing a blinding rainbow light. Nobita looks in happy surprise, his mouth open under his helmet. Screen freezes on Nobita's face and cuts to black abruptly!
