# Doraemon The Movie Trailer: "Nobitas großes Wolkenkönigreich" (The Cloud Kingdom Adventure) - Official Gadgets Edition

This document contains the complete script for a 120-second Doraemon Movie trailer (12 scenes, 10 seconds each) featuring official, legendary gadgets: **"The Anywhere Door" (ประตูสารพัดสถานที่)**, **"The Cloud Solidifying Spray" (สเปรย์เมฆแข็งตัว)**, and **"The Bamboo Copter" (คอปเตอร์ไม้ไผ่)**.

To strictly avoid safety filters, this script uses whimsical and magical descriptions with zero negative words, weapons, or fire. Every scene features dynamic camera directions and strict negative prompts to prevent unwanted hand-waving gestures.

## 🎬 Movie Outline: "Nobitas großes Wolkenkönigreich"
**Total Duration:** 120 seconds (12 scenes, 10 seconds each)

*   **Scene 1 (0-10s):** Nobita is lying comically on the tatami mats, wishing for a private kingdom in the sky. Doraemon smiles and pulls out the official **"Cloud Solidifying Spray"** (Wolken-Verfestigungsspray) and **"The Anywhere Door"** (Dokodemo-Tür).
*   **Scene 2 (10-20s):** They step through **"The Anywhere Door"** and arrive on a giant cloud in the blue sky. Doraemon uses the spray, and they happily jump on the solid cloud surface.
*   **Scene 3 (20-30s):** Nobita, Doraemon, Shizuka, Giant, and Suneo fly through the sky wearing official **"Bamboo Copters"** (Take-copter) on their heads, landing on the giant solid cloud, chatting excitedly.
*   **Scene 4 (30-40s):** Together, they build a massive, beautiful castle made of colorful fluffy clouds. Shizuka shapes cloud flowers, Suneo and Giant build cloud walls, laughing.
*   **Scene 5 (40-50s):** The Cloud Kingdom is complete, featuring rainbow bridges and glowing cloud towers. The five friends walk through their beautiful new kingdom, pointing at the views.
*   **Scene 6 (50-60s) [The Mystery]:** Suddenly, a golden celestial aurora appears in the sky. Right in the middle of their cloud kingdom, a mysterious, glowing ancient **"Sky Temple of Lights"** rises from the clouds.
*   **Scene 7 (60-70s) [The Flight]:** The five friends fly towards the glowing Sky Temple wearing their Bamboo Copters. The camera tracks them in a cinematic flying formation against the starry sky.
*   **Scene 8 (70-80s) [The Entrance]:** They land on the glowing crystal steps of the temple. The ancient doors are decorated with glowing star symbols. Nobita walks forward, looking amazed.
*   **Scene 9 (80-90s) [The Discovery]:** Inside the temple, they find a giant floating star crystal humming with magical neon blue light. Doraemon examines it with his magnifying glass.
*   **Scene 10 (90-100s) [The Lock]:** A golden pedestal rises from the temple floor with a keyhole. Doraemon pulls out an ancient glowing golden key. Giant and Suneo watch closely.
*   **Scene 11 (100-110s) [The Activation]:** Doraemon inserts the golden key. The crystal glows with a blinding, beautiful white-gold light. The temple roof opens, revealing a panoramic view of the starry galaxy.
*   **Scene 12 (110-120s) [Thai Drama Ending]:** Nobita stands close, looking up at the galaxy in happy surprise, his mouth open, his large round glasses slightly tilted in shock. The camera freezes instantly on Nobita's face, and then the screen cuts abruptly to black!
