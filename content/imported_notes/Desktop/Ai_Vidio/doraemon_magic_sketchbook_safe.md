# Doraemon Original Safe Episode Pack: "Der Zauber-Zeichenblock" (The Magic Sketchbook) - Group Version

This document contains the complete script for a 60-second Doraemon short video (6 scenes, 10 seconds each) featuring the original gadget: **"The Magic Sketchbook" (สมุดวาดเขียนวิเศษ)**. 

To maintain strict character consistency across all scenes in **labs.google**, each video prompt contains explicit details of all 5 main characters' features (face shape, hair, eyes, clothing, and signatures) so that the AI drawing styles match perfectly.

---

## 🎬 Story Outline: "Der Zauber-Zeichenblock" (Group Edition)
**Total Duration:** 60 seconds (6 scenes, 10 seconds each)

*   **Scene 1 (0-10s):** Nobita is sitting sadly at his desk, staring at a blank sheet of paper. Doraemon smiles and pulls out "The Magic Sketchbook" (Der Zauber-Zeichenblock).
*   **Scene 2 (10-20s):** In the backyard, Doraemon draws a giant magical toy treehouse in the book. The treehouse pops out of the page and becomes a real glowing wooden treehouse in the yard.
*   **Scene 3 (20-30s):** Shizuka, Giant, and Suneo walk into the backyard, looking up in wide-eyed wonder at the giant glowing toy treehouse. Nobita and Doraemon wave to them happily.
*   **Scene 4 (30-40s):** Shizuka takes a crayon and draws on the sketchbook. Beautiful glowing paper butterflies pop out of the page and flutter around the treehouse.
*   **Scene 5 (40-50s):** Giant and Suneo take the book. Giant draws a giant friendly blue balloon dinosaur, and Suneo draws a tiny spaceship. Both objects pop out and float around the yard.
*   **Scene 6 (50-60s):** All five friends (Doraemon, Nobita, Shizuka, Giant, Suneo) play happily on the lawn near the treehouse, surrounded by the floating balloon dinosaur, spaceship, and butterflies. They wave to the camera.
