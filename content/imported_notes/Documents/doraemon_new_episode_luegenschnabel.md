# Doraemon New Episode Pack: "Der Lügen-Schnabel" (The Lies-Become-Truth Beak)

This document contains the storyboard, character dialogues in German, and video prompts in English for a 70-second Doraemon short video (7 scenes, 10 seconds each). There is no Thai text in this document.

---

## 🎬 Story Outline: "Der Lügen-Schnabel" (The Lies-Become-Truth Beak)
*Total Duration: 70 seconds (7 scenes, 10 seconds each)*

* **Scene 1 (0-10s):** Nobita boasts to Giant and Suneo in the park about seeing a UFO, but they laugh at him and call him a liar.
* **Scene 2 (10-20s):** Nobita cries to Doraemon in his room. Doraemon pulls out the yellow "Lies-Become-Truth Beak" gadget.
* **Scene 3 (20-30s):** Nobita wears the beak and shouts in the park that a giant friendly toy robot will land. Giant and Suneo look skeptical.
* **Scene 4 (30-40s):** A giant colorful toy robot actually descends from the sky, leaving Suneo and Giant in complete shock.
* **Scene 5 (40-50s):** Nobita says it will rain candy, and colorful candies start raining from the sky. They all eat and laugh.
* **Scene 6 (50-60s):** Giant snatches the beak, wears it, and shouts that a giant gorilla will come and serve him tea.
* **Scene 7 (60-70s):** A giant gorilla in a butler suit walks out of the bushes carrying a tea tray. Suneo and Giant look terrified while Nobita and Doraemon laugh in the background. Screen freezes and cuts to black abruptly!
